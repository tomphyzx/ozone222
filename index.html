Model

Generation G = 2·σO₂·[O₂]·(photon rate)·L/V, with σO₂(222 nm) = 4.09×10⁻²⁴ cm², 2 O₃ per absorbed photon, 25 °C and 1 atm. Steady state: ΔO₃ = G / (ACH + kdep + k254), so required ACH = G/target − kdep − k254. 254 nm removal is k254 = Φ·σO₃(254)·(photon rate)·L254/V, with σO₃ = 1.13×10⁻¹⁷ cm².

Assumptions and limits

The room is well mixed and the gas is optically thin. Light is absorbed at the floor and walls, with no reflection. ACH means outdoor (ozone-free) air: recirculated air does not remove ozone unless it passes activated carbon. The result is ozone added on top of outdoor background. Module outputs are estimates from Ushio's on-axis irradiance and beam angle (B1 ≈ 0.11 W, B1.5 ≈ 0.05 W); measure or use the manufacturer's total-output figure if you have it. This tool does not check occupant exposure limits. Not a substitute for measurement.
